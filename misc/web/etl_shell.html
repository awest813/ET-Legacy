Click the game to capture the mouse. Esc releases it for menus.
If capture is blocked, hold the right mouse button to aim and left-click to fire.
Sound resumes on a click. The top bar controls volume and mute.
On a phone, touch controls turn on automatically. Use landscape for a larger view.

Game controls

Start with L to choose a team and class. In the team menu, point and click to make your selection.

Touch controls

Drag the left stick to move and the right side to aim. Hold Fire, Sprint or Crouch; tap Jump, Reload or Use. When wounded, Jump joins the reinforcement queue; waiting lets a Medic revive you. Alt fire switches supported weapon modes, including scopes and rifle grenades. Prone lies down or stands up. Team selects your class; Menu opens options; Weapon cycles weapons. Yes and No answer the current vote or fireteam prompt (F1/F2). Tap native menus to select. Landscape gives a larger view. Touch on/off switches controls.

Focus & sound

Click the game to capture the mouse. If capture is blocked, hold the right mouse button and drag to aim; left click fires. Use Capture mouse to retry capture.

Click, tap, or press a key in the game to resume sound. Use the top bar to mute or adjust volume. Volume changes keep your mute setting. Sound pauses while the game is out of focus; click the game to hear your adjustment. Press Shift + Tab from the game to reach these controls.

Return to match setup?

Returning ends your current match. Choose a new offline match or switch to online play in the launcher.
